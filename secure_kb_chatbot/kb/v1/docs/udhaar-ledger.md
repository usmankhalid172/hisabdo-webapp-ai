## What it is
Each customer has their own profile with name and contact details, their own transaction history and a running balance. HisabDo tracks money customers owe you (receivables) with the full history and the current outstanding balance.

## Recording udhar (money given)
Record the transaction against that customer as money given (udhar), with the date, the amount and an optional note such as "grocery items". The customer's running balance updates as the entry is recorded.

## Recording a payment received
Record it against the same customer as money received (payment). The outstanding balance goes down. Partial payments are recorded the same way.

## Sharing a statement
Generate a PDF statement for the customer showing what is owed, what has been paid and the running balance, and share it via WhatsApp, email or any messaging app.
