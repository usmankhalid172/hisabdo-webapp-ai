# Product Guidance & PDF Verification

## 1. Objective
Verify Expenses, Income, Khata, Udhar, Reports, PDF workflow, and multilingual support.

## 2. Practical User Guidance
Actual application access is required to verify the exact steps for each feature.

### Expenses
Verify adding an expense, entering amount and category, and saving the entry.

### Income
Verify adding income, entering amount and source, and saving the entry.

### Khata
Verify creating or opening a ledger and checking its entries and balance.

### Udhar
Verify recording credit or debt, selecting the relevant person, and checking outstanding balance.

### Reports
Verify opening reports, selecting date filters, checking totals, and generating a report.

## 3. PDF Workflow Test Cases
1. Open the relevant report.
2. Select PDF export or download.
3. Confirm the PDF downloads successfully.
4. Open the downloaded file.
5. Check that amounts, dates, labels, and totals are readable and correct.
