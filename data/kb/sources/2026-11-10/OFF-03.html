Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, detailed answers about HisabDo’s offline capability, AI voice recognition, data ownership, and reports.

Does HisabDo work 100% offline without the internet?

Yes! HisabDo is built from the ground up as an offline-first architecture. All your customer ledgers, daily transactions, expenses, and PDF statements work locally on your device without requiring mobile data or Wi-Fi. If you choose to sign in, your data can optionally synchronize to our secure cloud when connectivity becomes available.

How does AI Urdu Voice Entry work?

You simply tap the microphone button inside the app and speak your transaction naturally in Urdu, Roman Urdu, or English (for example: "Akram bhai ko 3,500 udhar diya"). HisabDo’s edge speech model parses the customer name, amount, and transaction type, and prepares the ledger entry in seconds.

Is HisabDo really free? Are there any hidden fees?

HisabDo is 100% free to download and use on Google Play. There are no mandatory monthly subscriptions, no locked transaction limits, and no hidden fees for exporting PDF reports or tracking unlimited customers.

How secure is my commercial financial data?

In guest mode, your data lives strictly in an encrypted SQLite database on your device; zero commercial records are sent to external servers. If you choose cloud backup, transactions are encrypted with industry-standard AES-256 protocols and stored securely on enterprise Supabase infrastructure.

Can I export reports and share them on WhatsApp?

Yes! With a single tap, you can generate clean, professional PDF account statements formatted in both English and Urdu. You can send them directly to customers or suppliers via WhatsApp, email, or print them on standard paper.

What happens if I change or lose my phone?

HisabDo provides an easy local backup and restore feature. You can save your encrypted database backup to your phone storage, Google Drive, or SD card and restore it on any Android device. Signed-in users can also restore their synced records automatically.

What is the relationship between HisabDo and XICTEK Systems?

HisabDo is a flagship software product of XICTEK Systems, an engineering company founded by Mian Usman Khalid. XICTEK Systems provides the long-term engineering, cloud infrastructure, AI models, and continuous product updates for HisabDo.
