Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about HisabDo — from offline data storage and AI Urdu voice recognition to WhatsApp PDF statements and cloud backup.

What is HisabDo and who is it designed for?

HisabDo is a free offline-first digital khata, customer ledger, and expense manager for Android. It is purpose-built for retail shopkeepers (kiryana, general stores, clothing, electronics), wholesalers, distributors, freelancers, tradesmen, and small business owners who need to track money given (udhar), payments received (wasool), daily store expenses, and generate professional PDF statements without relying on an active internet connection.

Is HisabDo completely free to use?

Yes, HisabDo is 100% free to download and use on Google Play. You get unlimited customer ledgers, unlimited daily credit/debit transactions, full expense tracking, and unlimited bilingual PDF statements with zero hidden fees, zero locked features, and zero monthly subscription bills.

Can I use HisabDo without creating an account or signing in?

Yes! HisabDo supports a frictionless "Guest Mode". You can immediately start logging sales, adding customers, and exporting reports right after installing the app without providing an email, phone number, or password. All data is saved directly to your phone’s local storage.

Where can I download the official HisabDo app?

HisabDo is officially distributed through the Google Play Store under package name "com.usman.hisabdo". You can search for "HisabDo" in Google Play or click any of the download buttons across this website.

What devices and Android versions are supported?

HisabDo is engineered with Flutter for extreme memory efficiency and supports virtually all Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above. It consumes less than 30MB of RAM and works smoothly even on budget smartphones.

Does HisabDo work without internet or cellular data?

Yes! HisabDo is strictly offline-first. All database queries, customer additions, voice transcriptions, cashflow calculations, and PDF generation happen 100% locally on your device. You can use it in underground basement markets, remote rural areas, or during internet outages without any interruption.

How is my commercial financial data protected?

In guest mode, your business transactions and customer credit books remain exclusively on your physical phone inside an encrypted SQLite database. Zero bytes of your commercial records are sent to outside ad brokers or data aggregators. If you opt into cloud backup, data is transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections to secure Supabase cloud infrastructure.

Does HisabDo sell merchant transaction records or show intrusive ads?

No. HisabDo strictly honors merchant data sovereignty. Your supplier prices, customer credit balances, and cash collections are your confidential business property. We do not sell user data or interrupt your daily bookkeeping with intrusive full-screen video ads.

Can I lock HisabDo with a fingerprint or PIN code?

Yes! HisabDo includes built-in app security settings allowing you to lock the app with your device’s biometric fingerprint sensor or a custom numerical PIN code, ensuring your staff or visitors cannot view your business numbers without authorization.

Can I delete all my data if I decide to stop using the app?

Yes. You maintain complete control. In guest mode, simply clearing app data or uninstalling the app purges all local records from your device immediately. Signed-in users can also request full account and cloud record deletion from settings or via our website’s Data Deletion page.

How does AI Urdu Voice Entry work in HisabDo?

When you tap the microphone button on the transaction screen and speak naturally in Urdu, Roman Urdu, or English (e.g. "Farhan ko char hazar ka samaan udhar diya"), our edge acoustic model isolates the customer entity ("Farhan"), the numeric value ("4,000"), and the transaction category ("Udhar / Given"). The parsed fields appear immediately for quick one-tap verification.

Does the voice recognition work in noisy market environments?

Yes. Our speech pipeline incorporates audio frequency normalization and ambient background noise filtering designed specifically for busy Pakistani bazaars, street traffic, and counter background chatter.

What languages and dialects are supported for voice entry?

HisabDo supports standard Urdu (اردو), Roman Urdu, Pakistani English, and common trade terms (such as "hazar", "lakh", "sau", "udhar", "wasool", "jama", "kharcha"). Support for Punjabi and regional dialects is continuously being expanded.

Can I still type transactions manually if I prefer not to speak?

Yes! Voice entry is completely optional. HisabDo includes a high-speed custom numeric keypad that lets you log a transaction manually in less than 3 taps.

Does voice data get recorded and saved on external servers?

No. The audio stream is parsed into text on the fly to extract numbers and names, and the temporary audio buffer is discarded immediately. No private store conversations are permanently archived.

How do I add and manage customer accounts?

Tap the "+ Add Customer" button. You can enter the customer’s name, phone number, and optional credit limit manually, or pick them directly from your phone’s contact list. Every customer receives a dedicated 360° ledger showing their entire transaction history.

Can I track money I owe to suppliers (Payables) as well as customer credit?

Yes! HisabDo features dual-sided bookkeeping. You can track "Maine Lene Hain" (Receivables from customers) and "Maine Dene Hain" (Payables to distributors and suppliers) in separate, clearly marked views.

How does HisabDo prevent calculation errors in running balances?

Unlike physical registers where manual addition mistakes are common, HisabDo automatically calculates the mathematical running balance with double-entry precision after every single transaction, guaranteeing 100% calculation accuracy.

Can I send automated payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. From any customer’s ledger page, tap the WhatsApp icon to generate a pre-formatted, polite payment reminder message containing the exact remaining balance and date of the last transaction.

Can I set credit limits for customers who borrow regularly?

Yes. You can assign a custom credit threshold to any customer. When their outstanding balance approaches or exceeds that amount, HisabDo alerts you so you can decide whether to extend further credit.

How do I generate a PDF account statement?

Open any customer account or go to the Reports section, select your desired date range (Today, This Week, This Month, or Custom Dates), and tap "Export PDF". The app generates a vector PDF file within milliseconds.

What information is included in the PDF statement?

The PDF statement includes your store name, contact details, customer name, beginning balance, an itemized table of every transaction with timestamps and notes, total received, total given, and the final certified balance.

Is the PDF bilingual in English and Urdu?

Yes! Statements feature bilingual headers in clear English and elegant Nastaliq Urdu, ensuring that customers of all literacy levels can easily verify their numbers.

Can I print the PDF report on standard office paper?

Yes. HisabDo exports standard A4 formatted PDF documents that are compatible with any mobile-connected printer (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) or desktop printer.

Can I attach photos of paper receipts or bills to transactions?

Yes. You can snap a photo of a physical paper bill, dispatch slip, or bank deposit slip using your phone camera and attach it directly to the transaction record.

How do I backup my local data in guest mode?

Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Tap "Create Local Backup". The app creates an encrypted backup snapshot file that you can save to your phone’s internal memory, an SD card, or upload to your personal Google Drive.

How does optional cloud synchronization work?

When you create a free HisabDo account and sign in, your device registers with our encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL cloud backend. Whenever your phone connects to the internet, background sync automatically replicates your transactions to the cloud with zero manual effort.

Can I use HisabDo on multiple phones with the same account?

Yes. By signing into the same HisabDo account on multiple devices, your customer ledgers and daily transactions synchronize across phones, allowing business partners or shop assistants to access the same ledger simultaneously.

How do I restore my records if I switch to a new phone?

On your new phone, install HisabDo from Google Play. If you used cloud sync, simply sign into your account and your records will restore automatically. If you used local backup, copy your backup file to the new phone and tap "Restore Local Backup".
