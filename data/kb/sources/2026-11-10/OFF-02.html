Systems Engineering

4-Tier Application Architecture

How HisabDo achieves zero-downtime offline durability with modern AI speech processing.

Tier 01

Presentation & Mobile Client Layer

Flutter & Dart Engine

Native 60fps Flutter framework with ultra-lightweight memory footprint (< 30MB RAM usage). Responsive on low-end Android 8+ devices up to modern flagship smartphones.

  • Native 60fps fluid UI animations
  • Dark mode & high-contrast outdoor readability
  • Custom numeric ledger keypad for rapid logging
  • Biometric fingerprint & PIN lock support
Tier 02

AI Voice & Entity Extraction Engine

On-Device NLP Pipeline

Lightweight acoustic and natural language pipeline tailored to South Asian dialects. Accurately extracts customer names, numbers, and transaction types from spoken speech.

  • Native Nastaliq Urdu & Roman Urdu speech model
  • Background ambient noise suppression for busy markets
  • Automatic numeric extraction (Hazar, Lakh, Sau)
  • Under 1-second edge inference time
Tier 03

Embedded Local Database Vault

Encrypted SQLite Engine

Zero-latency local relational storage. All customer accounts, transactions, and cashflow logs commit directly to device flash storage with zero internet connection required.

  • ACID-compliant atomic writes (0 data loss)
  • AES-256 local database encryption
  • Sub-millisecond query performance on 100K+ records
  • Exportable encrypted binary backup snapshots
Tier 04

Cloud & Optional Sync Infrastructure

Supabase PostgreSQL

When an optional account is signed in, background workers seamlessly reconcile local ledger mutations with enterprise cloud PostgreSQL using conflict-free hashing.

  • Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement
  • Automatic retry with exponential backoff on reconnection
  • Multi-device cross-synchronization
  • Real-time delta synchronization protocol

A Typical Business Day With HisabDo

08:00 AM — Opening Shop

Instant Cashflow & Dues Overview

Open the app and instantly see your opening cash-in-hand, total customer receivables, and supplier payables due today. The red and green indicator cards show your true financial health at a single glance without tallying yesterday’s register.

12:30 PM — Busy Counter Rush

Hands-Free Urdu Voice Logging

Customer buys Rs 4,500 worth of supplies while your hands are busy packing cartons. Tap the microphone and say "Babar bhai ko 4,500 udhar diya". HisabDo logs the entry into Babar’s ledger in under a second with zero keyboard typing.

04:15 PM — Customer Balance Inquiry

1-Click WhatsApp PDF Statement

A customer stops by asking "Mera pichla kitna hisab baqi hai?". Tap their name to open their 360-degree ledger. Generate a clean bilingual PDF receipt showing date-by-date entries, and share it directly to their WhatsApp before they leave.

09:00 PM — Closing the Cash Register

Daily Profit & Margin Audit

Review today’s total sales, cash collections, utility expenses, and net profit in colorful visual charts. Identify slow-paying debtors and schedule automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders for the morning.
