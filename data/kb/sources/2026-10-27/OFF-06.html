Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

HisabDo (“we”, “our”, or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the HisabDo mobile application (“App”) and website collect, use, store, and protect your information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the App.

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely by Supabase Authentication)

We do not store your password.

Business & Financial Data

When you use the App, you may create and store:

  • Customer names, phone numbers, and email addresses
  • Notes, income records, expense records, transaction amounts
  • Categories, dates, images attached to transactions
  • Business profile information

This information may be stored locally on your device. In signed-in mode, supported customer, income, and expense records may be synchronized with our cloud infrastructure through Supabase. The App does not claim that every locally stored category is synchronized to the cloud.

Device Information

The App may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs (if applicable)

This information is used only for improving app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Synchronize supported customer, income, and expense records across signed-in devices
  • Back up supported local records using the App's backup process
  • Restore your information after reinstalling the app
  • Improve app reliability
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cloud Storage & Synchronization

If you sign in to the App:

  • Supported customer, income, and expense records may be synchronized with our cloud infrastructure through Supabase.
  • The current cloud synchronization path is limited to those supported data types.
  • Cloud synchronization requires an internet connection and is optional for the signed-in experience.

Cloud synchronization is optional and requires signing into your account. The App does not currently describe all locally stored app data as cloud-synchronized.

4. Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device to provide fast offline access. Guest users can use the App without creating an account and keep supported records in local SQLite storage. Local data may include:

  • Customers, income, expenses
  • Settings, categories, business profile

The App is designed to work offline for supported local features. Guest users can continue in local/offline mode without Supabase synchronization. For signed-in users, supported customer, income, and expense records can synchronize with the cloud when connectivity is available.

5. Authentication

User authentication is managed using Supabase Authentication. Authentication may include email and password login. Passwords are never stored by the application itself.

Community blog submissions

If you create a contributor account or submit a blog article, Supabase processes your account email and authentication data. We store the display name and the article title, summary and text you submit so that we can review and manage the submission.

Pending and rejected submissions are visible only to the submitting account and authorized reviewers. If approved, the article and chosen display name become public on the website and may be indexed by search engines. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in an article.

You may edit or delete an unpublished submission through your contributor account. For removal of a published article or account-related deletion requests, contact hisabdo.app@gmail.com. Supabase processes this data to provide authentication, storage and database services.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted third-party services.

Supabase

Used for user authentication, secure cloud database, and data synchronization.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

7. Advertising & Cookies

Our website may use third-party advertising technology, including Google AdSense, to display ads. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize advertisements, measure ad performance, and provide relevant content based on your browsing activity.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to control or opt out of certain personalized advertising. You can also manage cookie settings in your browser or device settings. For more information about Google’s advertising privacy practices, please review Google’s privacy information and advertising policy.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • HTTPS encrypted communication
  • Secure authentication
  • Access control and database security rules
  • Local data isolation between different user accounts

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Cloud data associated with a signed-in account remains associated with your account until you delete your account or request deletion of your data.

Signing out of the App currently clears the local SQLite database on the device. Cloud data associated with the account is not deleted merely by signing out.

Local data stored on your device may also be removed if you uninstall the App, clear app storage, or reset/delete the local database.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To request deletion, contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

12. Internet Requirement

Some features require an internet connection, including:

  • Login and registration
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Account recovery

Supported local/offline guest functionality can be used without an internet connection. For signed-in users, supported customer, income, and expense records can synchronize with the cloud when connectivity is available.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our legal rights
  • With trusted service providers (such as Supabase) necessary to operate the App

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on our website. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:

  • HisabDo
  • Email: hisabdo.app@gmail.com
  • Website: https://hisabdo.app

Summary

By using HisabDo, you acknowledge that:

  • Your account is securely authenticated using Supabase Authentication.
  • If you sign in, supported customer, income, and expense records may be synchronized with cloud infrastructure through Supabase.
  • Guest mode can store supported records locally and use local/offline functionality without Supabase synchronization.
  • Local backup and restore operate on the device or selected local storage location and are not a cloud restore mechanism.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You remain the owner of your business data.
