About this app

HisabDo is a powerful Expense Tracker, Khata Book and Customer Ledger app designed for shopkeepers, freelancers, startups and small businesses. Track daily expenses, manage udhar accounts, monitor customer balances and simplify business accounting from one easy-to-use app.

EXPENSE TRACKER

Record and organize daily expenses with a simple interface. Track business expenses, personal expenses and spending patterns. Whether you need a Daily Expense Tracker or an Expense Management solution, HisabDo helps keep your financial records organized.

DIGITAL KHATA BOOK

Replace your traditional paper khata with a digital Khata Book. Record customer transactions, manage udhar accounts, track credit sales and maintain accurate records for every customer.

UDHAR MANAGEMENT

Keep track of money you have given or received. Manage customer udhar, record payments and monitor outstanding balances without relying on paper notebooks.

CUSTOMER LEDGER

Maintain a complete Customer Ledger with transaction history, payment records, outstanding balances and running account summaries. Easily check customer balances and previous transactions.

BUSINESS ACCOUNTING

HisabDo provides simple Business Accounting tools for shopkeepers, retailers, freelancers, traders, distributors, service providers and small businesses.

Track income, expenses, receivables and business transactions without complicated accounting software.

DAILY EXPENSE MANAGEMENT

Record expenses as they happen and maintain a clear history of your spending. Review your financial activity whenever you need it.

REPORTS

Generate PDF reports and review your financial records in an organized format. Use reports to understand expenses, customer balances and business transactions.
