## How do I handle a customer who refuses to pay?

Start by ensuring your records are complete and accurate. Then have a direct conversation with the customer. If the balance is significant and the customer continues to refuse, you may need to consider formal options such as a written demand or, for larger amounts, legal advice. Stopping further credit to that customer is an immediate practical step.
