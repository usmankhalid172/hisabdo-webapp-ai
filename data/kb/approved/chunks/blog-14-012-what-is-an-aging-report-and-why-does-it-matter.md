## What is an aging report and why does it matter?

An aging report categorizes your receivables or payables by how long they have been outstanding: current (0–30 days), 31–60 days, 61–90 days and 90+ days. For receivables, older balances are harder to collect. For payables, older balances may indicate overdue obligations. The aging breakdown tells you where to focus your attention.
