## Avoid Duplicate Payments

Duplicate payments are more common than most business owners realize, particularly when payments are made in cash or when multiple people handle payments. To prevent duplicates:

- Record every payment immediately when it is made
- Mark invoices as paid in your records as soon as payment is made
- If multiple people can make payments, ensure all payments are recorded in a single system
- Reconcile your records with supplier statements monthly
