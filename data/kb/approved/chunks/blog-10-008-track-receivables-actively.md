## Track Receivables Actively

Receivables — money clients owe you for completed work — are one of the most important numbers in your freelance finances. Know at all times how much is outstanding, from which clients and for how long. Overdue receivables are a cash flow problem waiting to happen.

Set a personal policy: follow up on any invoice that is more than seven days overdue. Most late payments are not intentional — clients are busy and invoices get overlooked. A polite reminder is usually all it takes.
