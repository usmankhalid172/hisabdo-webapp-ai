## Cash Flow Forecasting

Once you have a few months of cash flow data, you can start forecasting. A simple cash flow forecast projects your expected inflows and outflows for the next 4–8 weeks.

To build a basic forecast:

- List all expected inflows for the next month — confirmed orders, expected customer payments, etc.
- List all known outflows — rent due date, salary payment date, supplier payments due
- Calculate the expected net cash flow for each week
- Identify any weeks where outflows exceed inflows — these are potential cash shortfalls to plan for

A forecast does not need to be perfectly accurate to be useful. Even a rough forecast helps you anticipate problems and take action before they become crises.
