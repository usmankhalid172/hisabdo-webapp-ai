## Key Bookkeeping Concepts (part 3)

### Accounts Payable

Accounts payable (AP) is money you owe to suppliers, vendors or others for goods or services you have already received. Tracking AP helps you manage your payment obligations and avoid late payment penalties or damaged supplier relationships.
