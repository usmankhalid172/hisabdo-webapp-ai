## Data Storage: What You Should Know

When evaluating any financial app, it is worth understanding exactly where your data is stored and under what conditions it may be transmitted.

**Guest mode:** HisabDo stores supported guest-mode records locally on the device. Local data may be lost if the device is lost, its app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, so keep a separate backup of records you need to retain.

**Signed-in mode:** Supported customer, income and expense records may synchronize with Supabase when connected. This is distinct from the app's backup and restore process for local records; the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) describes the current scope. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Neither approach is the right fit for every user. Guest mode keeps supported records local; signed-in synchronization adds connected access for supported record types. Choose based on your workflow and backup needs.
