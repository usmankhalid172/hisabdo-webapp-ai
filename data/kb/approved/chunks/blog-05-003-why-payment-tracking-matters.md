## Why Payment Tracking Matters

Accurate payables tracking has several important benefits:

- **Avoid missed payments:** Missing a payment damages your relationship with the supplier and may result in late fees or loss of credit terms.
- **Avoid duplicate payments:** Without records, it is easy to pay the same invoice twice — especially when payments are made in installments.
- **Cash flow planning:** Knowing when payments are due helps you plan your cash position and avoid shortfalls.
- **Dispute resolution:** If a supplier claims you owe more than you believe, accurate records allow you to verify the correct balance.
- **Supplier relationships:** Suppliers who are paid reliably and on time are more likely to offer better terms, priority service and flexibility during difficult periods.
