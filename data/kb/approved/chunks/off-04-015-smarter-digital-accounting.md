## Smarter Digital Accounting

HisabDo brings Expense Tracking, Khata Book, Udhar Management and Customer Ledger features together in one simple application.

Instead of using separate apps for expenses and customer accounts, manage your everyday financial records from one place.

Whether you are running a shop, managing a small business, working as a freelancer or tracking personal expenses, HisabDo helps simplify everyday financial management.
