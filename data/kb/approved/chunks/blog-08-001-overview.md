## Overview

Practical habits and routines that keep your daily ledger accurate, organized and easy to review — whether you run a shop, freelance or manage household finances.

Keeping a daily khata is one of the oldest financial habits in South Asian business culture. Whether you run a small grocery store, a tailoring shop or a home-based business, the khata — your daily record of money given and received — is the backbone of your financial awareness. Yet most people still rely on paper notebooks that get lost, torn or become impossible to read after a few months.

This guide walks you through practical steps to manage your daily khata more effectively, whether you use a paper register or a digital app like HisabDo.
