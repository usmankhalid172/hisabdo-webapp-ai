## What expenses can freelancers typically track as business costs?

Common freelance business expenses include software subscriptions and tools, hardware and equipment used for work, internet and phone bills (business portion), professional development courses, marketing costs, transport to client meetings and professional services like accounting. What qualifies as a deductible business expense varies by jurisdiction — consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your location and situation.
