## Using HisabDo for AR and AP Management

HisabDo is designed to make AR and AP management simple and accessible. Each customer and supplier has their own account with a complete transaction history and running balance. You can see at a glance what each customer owes you and what you owe each supplier, without any manual calculation.

The app's reporting features allow you to generate summaries of your total receivables and payables at any time, giving you the information you need to manage your cash flow effectively.
