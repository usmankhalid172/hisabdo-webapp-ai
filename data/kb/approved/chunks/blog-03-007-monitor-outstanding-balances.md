## Monitor Outstanding Balances

Knowing the balance is not enough — you also need to know how long each balance has been outstanding. A balance that is 7 days old is very different from one that is 90 days old.

Categorize your outstanding dues by age:

- 0–30 days: Current — normal credit period
- 31–60 days: Follow up gently
- 61–90 days: Follow up firmly
- 90+ days: Escalate — consider stopping further credit

This aging analysis helps you prioritize your collection efforts and identify customers who are becoming a credit risk.
