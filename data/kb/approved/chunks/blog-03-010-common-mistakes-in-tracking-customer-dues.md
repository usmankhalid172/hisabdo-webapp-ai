## Common Mistakes in Tracking Customer Dues

- **Not recording small dues:** A Rs. 500 due feels too small to bother with, but ten such dues add up to Rs. 5,000.
- **Relying on memory:** Memory is unreliable. Always record in writing or digitally.
- **Not recording partial payments:** This creates balance discrepancies and customer disputes.
- **Giving more credit to customers with existing overdue balances:** This compounds the problem.
- **Not following up consistently:** Customers who are not followed up on tend to deprioritize payment.
- **Mixing customer dues with general income records:** Keep receivables tracking separate and detailed.
