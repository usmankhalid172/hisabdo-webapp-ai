## Does HisabDo work without internet for expense tracking?

Supported local expense features can be used without an internet connection. Guest-mode records stay on the device; supported expense records may synchronize with Supabase in signed-in mode. See the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for details.
