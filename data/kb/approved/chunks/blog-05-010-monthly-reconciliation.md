## Monthly Reconciliation

At the end of each month, reconcile your payables records with your suppliers. This means comparing your records of what you owe each supplier with the supplier's records of what you owe them.

Discrepancies can arise from timing differences (a payment you made on the last day of the month may not appear in the supplier's records until the following month), recording errors, or genuine disputes. Reconciling monthly catches these discrepancies early, before they become significant problems.
