## Overview

Track what you owe, pay on time and maintain strong supplier relationships — with a simple payables management system for small businesses.

Most small business owners focus on collecting money from customers — and rightly so. But the other side of the ledger matters just as much. Knowing exactly what you owe to suppliers, when payments are due and what you have already paid is essential for maintaining good supplier relationships and managing your cash flow effectively.

Poor payables management leads to missed payments, duplicate payments, damaged supplier relationships and cash flow surprises. A simple, consistent system prevents all of these problems.
