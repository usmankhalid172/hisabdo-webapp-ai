## Can HisabDo help with cash flow management?

Yes. HisabDo helps you track all income and expenses, manage customer receivables and supplier payables, and generate monthly reports. Having accurate, up-to-date records is the foundation of cash flow management. The app's reports give you a clear picture of your cash position at any time.
