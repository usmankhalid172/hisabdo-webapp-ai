## 3. Accuracy of Records

You are solely responsible for ensuring that the records you maintain in HisabDo are accurate, complete and compliant with any applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

HisabDo shall not be liable for any financial loss, tax liability, legal consequence or other harm arising from inaccurate, incomplete or incorrectly entered records.
