## Receivables Reports: What to Include

A receivables report should contain:

- Report date
- Each customer with an outstanding balance
- The outstanding amount for each customer
- How long each balance has been outstanding (aging)
- Total outstanding receivables

The aging breakdown — current (0–30 days), 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days — is particularly important. Older balances are harder to collect and represent greater risk to your cash flow.
