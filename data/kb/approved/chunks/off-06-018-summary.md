## Summary

By using HisabDo, you acknowledge that:

- Your account is securely authenticated using Supabase Authentication.
- If you sign in, supported customer, income, and expense records may be synchronized with cloud infrastructure through Supabase.
- Guest mode can store supported records locally and use local/offline functionality without Supabase synchronization.
- Local backup and restore operate on the device or selected local storage location and are not a cloud restore mechanism.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- You remain the owner of your business data.
