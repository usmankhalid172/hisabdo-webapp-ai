## 3. Expense Tracking

Expense tracking is the systematic recording of every cost your business incurs. This is where most small businesses have the biggest gaps — expenses are often recorded inconsistently or not at all.

Record every expense immediately when it occurs. Categorize it. Keep the receipt or note the details. Review your expense totals weekly.

The most important rule: no expense is too small to record. Small expenses add up to significant amounts over a month.
