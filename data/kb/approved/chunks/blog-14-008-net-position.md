## Net Position

Your net position is the difference between your total receivables and your total payables. If your receivables exceed your payables, you are in a net asset position — more money is owed to you than you owe to others. If your payables exceed your receivables, you are in a net liability position.

Monitoring your net position regularly gives you a quick snapshot of your business's financial health and helps you anticipate cash flow needs.
