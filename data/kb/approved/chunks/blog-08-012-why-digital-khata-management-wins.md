## Why Digital Khata Management Wins

Paper khata registers have served businesses for generations, but they have real limitations: they can be lost, damaged or stolen. They cannot calculate totals automatically. They cannot send you reminders about overdue balances. And they cannot generate a professional PDF report to share with a customer or accountant.

A digital khata app can make entries easier to search and balances easier to review. HisabDo is designed offline-first for supported local features. Guest-mode records remain on the device, while supported records in signed-in mode may synchronize with Supabase. Offline access is not itself a backup, so keep an independent backup if the records are important to your business.
