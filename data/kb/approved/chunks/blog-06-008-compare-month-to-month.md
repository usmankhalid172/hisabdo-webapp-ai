## Compare Month to Month

Monthly comparisons are one of the most powerful tools in expense management. When you compare this month's expenses to last month's, you can quickly spot trends — rising costs, seasonal patterns and one-time expenses that should not recur.

HisabDo's analytics features help you visualize these patterns without manual calculation, giving you a clear picture of your business's financial health at a glance.
