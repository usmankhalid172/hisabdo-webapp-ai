## Know When to Stop Extending Credit

Some customers are consistently late payers or have balances that keep growing without regular payments. At some point, it is better to stop extending credit to these customers — or to require cash payment — rather than continuing to accumulate uncollectable debt. This is a difficult conversation but an important one for your business's health.
