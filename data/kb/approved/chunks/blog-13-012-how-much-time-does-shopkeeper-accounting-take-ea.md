## How much time does shopkeeper accounting take each day?

With a consistent system, daily accounting takes 10–15 minutes — a brief morning review of yesterday's totals and recording transactions throughout the day as they happen. The evening closing routine takes another 10–15 minutes. The weekly review takes 30 minutes. Most of the time is saved by recording transactions immediately rather than trying to reconstruct them later.
