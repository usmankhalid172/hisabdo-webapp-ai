## Record Every Purchase on Credit

When a supplier delivers goods or services on credit, record the transaction immediately. Include:

- Date of delivery
- Supplier name
- Description of goods or services received
- Amount owed
- Payment due date (based on your agreed terms)

Do not wait until the invoice arrives to record the transaction. Record it when the goods are received. If the invoice amount differs from what you expected, note the discrepancy and resolve it with the supplier.
