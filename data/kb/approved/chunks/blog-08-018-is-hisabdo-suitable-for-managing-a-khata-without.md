## Is HisabDo suitable for managing a khata without internet?

Supported local features can be used without internet. Guest-mode records are stored on the device; supported customer, income and expense records may synchronize in signed-in mode. Please consult the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for the current details.
