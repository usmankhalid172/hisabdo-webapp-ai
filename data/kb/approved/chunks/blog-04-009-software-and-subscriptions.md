## Software and Subscriptions

In today's business environment, software subscriptions are a common expense that many businesses underestimate:

- Business apps and tools
- Cloud storage
- Design or productivity software
- Accounting or bookkeeping apps
- Communication tools

Review your software subscriptions quarterly. It is easy to accumulate subscriptions that are no longer actively used. Cancelling unused subscriptions is one of the quickest ways to reduce costs.
