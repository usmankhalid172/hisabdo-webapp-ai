## Categorize Your Expenses

The first step is to organize your expenses into categories. Common categories for small businesses include:

- Inventory and stock purchases
- Rent and utilities
- Staff wages and salaries
- Transport and delivery
- Marketing and advertising
- Equipment and maintenance
- Miscellaneous and petty cash

Having clear categories makes it easy to spot which areas are consuming the most resources and where you have room to cut back.
