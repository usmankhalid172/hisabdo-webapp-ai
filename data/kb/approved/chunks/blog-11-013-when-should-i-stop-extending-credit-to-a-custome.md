## When should I stop extending credit to a customer?

Consider stopping further credit when a customer's balance has been overdue for more than 60 days without a payment plan, when their balance keeps growing without regular payments, or when they are unresponsive to follow-up. Stopping credit is not about damaging the relationship — it is about protecting your business while you work to resolve the existing balance.
