## How do I decide how much credit to extend to a customer?

Consider the customer's payment history with you, the size and frequency of their purchases and your own cash flow position. Start with a conservative credit limit for new customers and increase it as they demonstrate reliable payment. There is no universal formula — the right limit depends on your business and your relationship with each customer.
