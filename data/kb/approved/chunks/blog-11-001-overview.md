## Overview

How to set credit policies, manage credit limits, handle overdue accounts and maintain strong customer relationships — without letting unpaid balances damage your business.

Extending credit to customers is a common practice in South Asian business culture. Whether it is a shopkeeper allowing a regular customer to take goods on account, a supplier providing stock on credit terms or a service provider billing after delivery, credit relationships are central to how many businesses operate.

But poorly managed credit is one of the most common causes of cash flow problems for small businesses. This guide covers practical strategies for managing customer credit effectively — maintaining good relationships while protecting your business's financial health.
