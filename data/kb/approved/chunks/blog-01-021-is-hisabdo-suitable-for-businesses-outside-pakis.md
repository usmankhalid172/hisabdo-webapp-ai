## Is HisabDo suitable for businesses outside Pakistan?

Yes. HisabDo supports multiple languages including English, Hindi and Arabic, and the financial management principles it supports are universal. The app is used by small businesses and individuals across Pakistan, India and the broader South Asian diaspora.
