## How do I handle cash discrepancies at the end of the day?

When your physical cash does not match your recorded cash sales, first check for missing entries — a transaction that was not recorded. If you find the missing entry, record it and recount. If the discrepancy persists, note it as a cash variance and investigate the cause. Common causes include change errors, unrecorded small sales and petty cash that was not logged.
