## Why is it important to track both receivables and payables?

Tracking only receivables gives you an incomplete picture. Your net cash position depends on both what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers. A business with Rs. 100,000 in receivables but Rs. 120,000 in payables is in a net liability position despite having strong receivables.
