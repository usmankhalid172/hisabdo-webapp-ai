## Getting Started Today

You do not need to overhaul your entire system at once. Start by picking one habit from this list — perhaps recording every transaction the same day — and practice it consistently for two weeks. Once it feels natural, add another habit. Small, consistent improvements compound into a reliable financial system over time.
