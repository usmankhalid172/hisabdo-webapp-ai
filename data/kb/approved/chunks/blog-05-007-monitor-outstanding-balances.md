## Monitor Outstanding Balances

Review your total outstanding payables regularly. Know:

- Total amount owed across all suppliers
- Which payments are due this week
- Which payments are due next week
- Any overdue payments that need immediate attention

This overview helps you plan your cash outflows and ensures that no payment is missed or forgotten.
