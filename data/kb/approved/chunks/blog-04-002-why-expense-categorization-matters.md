## Why Expense Categorization Matters

When you categorize expenses consistently, you can answer questions like: How much did I spend on transport this month compared to last month? What percentage of my revenue goes to staff costs? Which expense category grew the most in the past quarter?

Without categories, these questions are impossible to answer without manually reviewing every transaction. With categories, the answers are available in seconds — either from a report or a simple filter in your expense tracker.

Categorization also makes it easier to identify waste. If you can see that your miscellaneous expenses have doubled in three months, that is a signal to investigate. If you cannot see it, the waste continues unnoticed.
