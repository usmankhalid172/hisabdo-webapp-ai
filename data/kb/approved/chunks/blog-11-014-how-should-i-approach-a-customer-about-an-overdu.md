## How should I approach a customer about an overdue balance?

Be direct but respectful. Frame the conversation around the balance and a payment plan, not around blame. Most customers who are late are not deliberately avoiding payment — they may be facing their own cash flow challenges. Offering a structured payment plan often resolves the situation better than demanding immediate full payment.
