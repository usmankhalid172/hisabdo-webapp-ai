## Replace Your Paper Khata With a Smarter System

HisabDo is designed to replace traditional paper registers with a modern, organized digital ledger. Guest users can keep supported records local on their device, and signed-in users can choose optional cloud synchronization when internet connectivity is available.

- Track money given and received in real time
- Manage customers and their complete history
- Export professional PDF reports anytime
- Use supported guest/local features without internet access
