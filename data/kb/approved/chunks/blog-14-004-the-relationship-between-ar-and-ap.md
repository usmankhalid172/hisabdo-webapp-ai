## The Relationship Between AR and AP

The relationship between your receivables and payables is central to your business's cash flow. Ideally, you want to collect from customers faster than you pay your suppliers. This creates a positive cash flow cycle where money comes in before it needs to go out.

When the opposite happens — when you pay suppliers before collecting from customers — you experience a cash flow gap that must be covered by your cash reserves or borrowing. Managing this gap is one of the most important financial management tasks for any business.
