## Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using too many categories:** More than 20 categories becomes difficult to manage consistently.
- **Using too few categories:** Putting everything into 3 or 4 categories makes analysis impossible.
- **Inconsistent categorization:** Recording the same type of expense in different categories at different times makes your data unreliable.
- **Ignoring small expenses:** Small expenses add up. Record and categorize everything.
- **Mixing personal and business expenses:** This distorts your business cost picture and makes records unreliable.
