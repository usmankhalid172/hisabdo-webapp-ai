## 7. Back Up Your Records Regularly

If you use a digital app, enable backup features and use them consistently. If you use paper, consider photographing your register pages weekly and storing them in a cloud folder. Losing months of khata records to a damaged notebook or a broken phone is a painful and avoidable problem.
