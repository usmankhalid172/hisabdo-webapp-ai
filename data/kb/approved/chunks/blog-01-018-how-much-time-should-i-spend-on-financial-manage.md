## How much time should I spend on financial management each week?

For most small businesses, 30–45 minutes per week is sufficient for daily recording (5–10 minutes per day) plus a weekly review (15–20 minutes). The monthly review takes 30–60 minutes. The key is making it a consistent habit rather than an occasional task.
