## When to Hire an Accountant

Good bookkeeping reduces the cost of professional accounting because your records are already organized and accurate. An accountant can then focus on higher-value work: tax planning, financial analysis and strategic advice. Consider hiring an accountant when your business grows to the point where tax complexity, regulatory requirements or financial analysis needs exceed what you can handle yourself.
