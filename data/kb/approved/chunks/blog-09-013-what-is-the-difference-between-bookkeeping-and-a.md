## What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping is the recording of financial transactions — every sale, expense, payment and receipt. Accounting is the interpretation and analysis of those records to understand financial performance, prepare statements and support decisions. Good bookkeeping is the prerequisite for good accounting. Without accurate records, accounting analysis is unreliable.
