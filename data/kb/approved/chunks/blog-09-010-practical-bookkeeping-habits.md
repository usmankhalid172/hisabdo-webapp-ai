## Practical Bookkeeping Habits

- Record every transaction the day it happens
- Keep receipts and supporting documents for all significant expenses
- Reconcile your records with your bank statement monthly
- Review your income and expense totals weekly
- Back up your records regularly
- Separate business and personal finances
