## Monthly Cash Flow Review

At the end of each month, review your cash flow for the month:

- Total cash received (all inflows)
- Total cash paid out (all outflows)
- Net cash flow (inflows minus outflows)
- Opening cash balance (start of month)
- Closing cash balance (end of month)

Compare this month to previous months. Is your cash position improving or deteriorating? Are there patterns — months where cash is consistently tight? Understanding these patterns helps you plan ahead.
