## Does HisabDo share my financial data with third parties?

HisabDo can be used as a guest with supported records stored locally on the device. When you sign in, supported data can synchronize with cloud infrastructure through Supabase. See our [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for full details about account, app and service data handling.
