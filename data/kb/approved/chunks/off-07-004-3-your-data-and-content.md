## 3. Your Data and Content

You retain ownership of all financial data and content you enter into HisabDo. The App stores this data locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the accuracy of your records and for backing up your data regularly.

HisabDo does not claim ownership of your financial records. However, by using the App, you acknowledge that the security of locally stored data depends on your device's own security measures.

You are solely responsible for the accuracy of the financial records you maintain in the App. HisabDo is a tool to assist with record-keeping and does not verify, audit or guarantee the accuracy of any data you enter.
