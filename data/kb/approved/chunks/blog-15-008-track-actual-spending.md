## Track Actual Spending

A budget is only useful if you compare it to your actual spending. Record every expense throughout the month and compare your actual spending to your budget at the end of each week. This comparison reveals where your plan is working and where you need to adjust.

HisabDo makes this tracking simple. Record each expense as it happens, categorize it and review your totals at any time. The app works offline, so you can record expenses even when you do not have internet access.
