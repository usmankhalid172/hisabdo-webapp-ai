## Common Budgeting Mistakes to Avoid

- Setting unrealistic targets that you cannot maintain
- Forgetting irregular expenses like annual fees or seasonal costs
- Not tracking small daily purchases that add up significantly
- Giving up after one bad month instead of adjusting and continuing
- Budgeting for income you expect but have not yet received
