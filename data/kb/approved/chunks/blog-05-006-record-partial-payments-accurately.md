## Record Partial Payments Accurately

Many supplier payments are made in installments. When you make a partial payment, record it immediately with the date and amount. Update the outstanding balance.

This is especially important when you have multiple outstanding invoices with the same supplier. Be clear about which invoice each payment applies to, or whether it is a general payment against the total balance.

Always get a receipt or acknowledgment from the supplier when you make a payment, and keep this for your records.
