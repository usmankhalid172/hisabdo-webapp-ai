## The 50/30/20 Framework

A commonly referenced budgeting framework divides income into three broad categories: roughly half for needs (housing, food, utilities, transport), a portion for wants (dining out, entertainment, hobbies) and a portion for savings and debt repayment. This is one possible starting point, not a universal rule. The right allocation depends entirely on your specific income, cost of living and financial obligations. Use it as a rough guide, not a fixed target.
