## 11. Digital Record Keeping

Digital record keeping can make calculations and searches easier and can support reports and backups. Access from multiple devices depends on the product's sync features, internet connection and the records it supports. Keep a separate backup plan for important business records.

For small businesses in Pakistan and South Asia, the transition from paper khata to digital is often the single most impactful improvement in financial management. The key is choosing a tool that is simple enough to use consistently.
