## 9. Data Retention

Cloud data associated with a signed-in account remains associated with your account until you delete your account or request deletion of your data.

Signing out of the App currently clears the local SQLite database on the device. Cloud data associated with the account is not deleted merely by signing out.

Local data stored on your device may also be removed if you uninstall the App, clear app storage, or reset/delete the local database.
