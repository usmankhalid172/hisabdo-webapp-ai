## 5. Follow Up on Overdue Balances Weekly

Your khata is only useful if you act on it. Set aside time each week — Friday evening works well for many shopkeepers — to review which customers have outstanding balances and for how long. Politely following up on overdue amounts keeps your cash flow healthy and prevents small debts from becoming large ones.
