## Track Every Client and Project Separately

Create a separate account or record for each client in your financial system. This allows you to see at a glance what each client owes you, what they have paid and the complete history of your financial relationship. When a client questions an invoice, you can show them the full record immediately.

HisabDo's customer management feature is ideal for this purpose. Each client gets their own profile with a complete transaction history and running balance.
