## What "Offline-First" Actually Means

An offline-first app keeps local data available for at least some tasks when there is no internet connection. The exact features and records that remain available differ by product, so check which actions work offline and what happens when connectivity returns.

This is different from "offline mode" in some cloud apps, which is a fallback that may have limited functionality or may not sync reliably when connectivity returns.

HisabDo supports local offline use and optional signed-in synchronization. Guest-mode records are stored on the device. In signed-in mode, supported customer, income and expense records may synchronize with Supabase; they are not described as an automatic cloud backup for every item. See the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for the current scope.
