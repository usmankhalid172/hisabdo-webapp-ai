## Maintain Good Relationships

Credit management is ultimately about relationships. The goal is not to be aggressive or suspicious, but to maintain clear, honest communication about financial obligations. Customers who feel respected and fairly treated are more likely to pay promptly and to continue doing business with you long-term.
