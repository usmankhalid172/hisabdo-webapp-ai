## 2. Income Tracking

Income tracking means recording every rupee that comes into your business. This includes cash sales, credit sales, advance payments and any other income.

For each income entry, record:

- Date received
- Amount
- Source (which customer, which product/service)
- Payment method (cash, bank transfer, etc.)

Track income daily. Waiting until the end of the week means you will miss entries and lose accuracy. Your total monthly income figure should match your actual cash received plus any new credit sales.
