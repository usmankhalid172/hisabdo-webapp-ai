## Expense Reports: What to Include

A useful expense report should contain:

- Report period
- Each expense entry with date, category, description and amount
- Total expenses for the period
- Subtotals by category
- Comparison to previous period or budget (optional)

Expense reports organized by category are far more useful than a simple chronological list. Seeing that you spent Rs. 45,000 on transport this month versus Rs. 28,000 last month is actionable information. A list of individual transactions is not.
