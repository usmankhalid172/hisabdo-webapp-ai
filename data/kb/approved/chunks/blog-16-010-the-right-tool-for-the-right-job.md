## The Right Tool for the Right Job

This is not an argument against cloud technology in general. Cloud tools are excellent for collaboration, for sharing data across multiple devices and for applications where real-time synchronization is essential. But for personal and small business financial records — where reliability, performance and local data control matter — offline-first is a strong architectural choice for many users.

HisabDo is designed for local offline use of supported features, with optional signed-in synchronization for supported record types when connected.
