## Weekly Receivables Review

Set aside 15–20 minutes each week to review your outstanding receivables. Look at:

- Total outstanding balance across all customers
- Which customers have balances older than 30 days
- Which customers have not made any payment in the past two weeks
- Any new credit sales from the past week

This weekly review keeps you aware of your receivables position and ensures that overdue balances do not go unnoticed for weeks at a time.
