## Keep Receipts and Notes

For every significant expense, keep the receipt or at minimum note the vendor, amount and purpose. This documentation is valuable if you ever need to dispute a charge, prepare for tax purposes or review your spending patterns.

HisabDo allows you to attach notes to expense entries, making it easy to add context without carrying a folder of paper receipts.
