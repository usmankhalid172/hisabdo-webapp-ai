## Managing Receivables for Better Cash Flow

Receivables management is one of the most powerful levers for improving cash flow. The faster you collect from customers, the better your cash position.

Practical steps:

- Set clear credit terms with customers — how long they have to pay
- Follow up on overdue balances promptly and consistently
- Offer small incentives for early payment where appropriate
- Stop extending further credit to customers with significantly overdue balances
- Review your total outstanding receivables weekly

See the related article on [tracking customer dues](https://hisabdo.app/blog/track-customer-dues) for a detailed system.
