## Common Cash Flow Mistakes

- **Confusing profit with cash:** Profit is an accounting concept. Cash is what you can actually spend.
- **Not tracking receivables:** Untracked dues mean you do not know your real cash position.
- **Ignoring seasonal patterns:** Businesses with seasonal revenue need to plan for slow periods during strong ones.
- **No cash reserve:** Operating without any cash buffer means any unexpected expense creates a crisis.
- **Paying expenses before collecting from customers:** Where possible, collect before you pay.
