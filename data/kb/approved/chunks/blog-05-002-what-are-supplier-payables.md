## What Are Supplier Payables?

Supplier payables — also called accounts payable — are amounts your business owes to suppliers for goods or services you have already received but not yet paid for. When a supplier delivers stock on credit, the amount owed is a payable.

In a traditional khata system, this is the udhar you have taken from your supplier. In accounting terms, it is a liability — money you owe that will need to be paid.

Managing payables well means knowing at any moment: which suppliers you owe money to, how much you owe each one, and when each payment is due.
