## What is the difference between operating expenses and cost of goods sold?

Cost of goods sold (COGS) is the direct cost of producing or purchasing the products you sell — materials, inventory, direct labor. Operating expenses are the costs of running the business that are not directly tied to production — rent, utilities, marketing, administration. Both are important, but they serve different analytical purposes.
