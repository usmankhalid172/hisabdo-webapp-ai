## Use Reports to Identify Patterns

Regular reports on your receivables reveal patterns that are not obvious from day-to-day management. Which customers always pay on time? Which ones are consistently late? Which ones have balances that keep growing? This information helps you make better decisions about who to extend credit to and on what terms.
