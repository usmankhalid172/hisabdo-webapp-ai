## The Aging Report

An aging report categorizes your receivables or payables by how long they have been outstanding: current (less than 30 days), 30-60 days, 60-90 days and more than 90 days. This report is one of the most useful tools in AR and AP management because it immediately shows you where attention is needed.

For receivables, older balances are harder to collect and represent greater risk. For payables, older balances may indicate overdue obligations that could damage supplier relationships.
