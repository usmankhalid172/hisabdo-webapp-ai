## Paper Khata vs. HisabDo

### Paper Register

Can be lost, damaged or hard to read. Totals are calculated by hand. Only one person can realistically maintain it. No backup exists if it's misplaced.

### HisabDo

Guest-mode records are stored locally, with backup and restore available for supported records. Signed-in users may synchronize supported data. Balances update as entries are recorded, and statements can be shared from the app.
