## 1. Financial Management Basics

Financial management is the process of planning, organizing, controlling and monitoring your business's financial resources. For a small business, this means:

- Knowing how much money is coming in and going out
- Tracking what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers
- Maintaining accurate records of all transactions
- Reviewing your financial position regularly
- Making decisions based on actual financial data, not guesswork

Good financial management does not require an accounting degree. It requires consistency, discipline and the right tools.
