## Offline-First Design

One concern many business owners have about digital tools is internet dependency. HisabDo is designed offline-first, and supported local features can be used without a connection. Guest-mode records are stored on the device; signed-in users may synchronize supported records with Supabase. Check the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for the current scope. A digital record still needs a backup plan, since data kept only on one device can be lost if that device is damaged or reset.
