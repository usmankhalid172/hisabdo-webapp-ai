## Use Technology to Reduce Friction

The biggest barrier to consistent expense tracking is friction — the effort required to record each entry. The easier you make the recording process, the more consistently you will do it. HisabDo's voice entry feature lets you record expenses by speaking, which takes seconds and removes the main reason people skip entries.
