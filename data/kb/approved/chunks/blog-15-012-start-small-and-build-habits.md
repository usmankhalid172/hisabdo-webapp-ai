## Start Small and Build Habits

You do not need a perfect budget from day one. Start by simply tracking your spending for one month without trying to change anything. This baseline data will reveal your actual spending patterns and give you the information you need to build a realistic budget.

Once you have that baseline, make one or two small adjustments — perhaps reducing dining out by 20% or setting up an automatic transfer to savings on payday. Small, consistent changes compound into significant financial improvement over time.
