## Review Outstanding Balances Weekly

Set aside time each week to review which customers have outstanding balances and for how long. Categorize them by age: current (less than 30 days), overdue (30-60 days) and seriously overdue (more than 60 days). The older a balance, the harder it typically is to collect.
