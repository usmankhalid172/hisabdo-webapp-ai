## Which Is Right for You?

A paper ledger may be sufficient for a small number of transactions and a workflow that one person can maintain. A digital ledger may be more convenient when searching across many accounts or preparing repeat statements. Consider device access, backup procedures, training time and whether the app covers your actual workflow before switching.

Some businesses use both during a transition: they compare balances, keep the paper register as a temporary reference and decide when the digital record is reliable enough to become primary. The right method depends on the business.
