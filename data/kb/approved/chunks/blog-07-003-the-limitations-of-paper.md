## The Limitations of Paper

Despite these advantages, paper ledgers have serious limitations that become more painful as a business grows:

- **No automatic calculations:** Every total must be computed manually, creating opportunities for arithmetic errors.
- **No search function:** Finding a specific customer's history means flipping through pages.
- **Damage and loss risk:** A single fire, flood or misplaced notebook can destroy months of records.
- **No reports:** Generating a monthly summary requires hours of manual work.
- **Illegibility:** Handwriting fades, smudges and becomes unreadable over time.
- **No reminders:** Paper cannot alert you when a customer's balance has been outstanding for 30 days.
