## 4. Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device to provide fast offline access. Guest users can use the App without creating an account and keep supported records in local SQLite storage. Local data may include:

- Customers, income, expenses
- Settings, categories, business profile

The App is designed to work offline for supported local features. Guest users can continue in local/offline mode without Supabase synchronization. For signed-in users, supported customer, income, and expense records can synchronize with the cloud when connectivity is available.
