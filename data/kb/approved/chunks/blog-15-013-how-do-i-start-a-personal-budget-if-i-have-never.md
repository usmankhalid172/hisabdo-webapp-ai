## How do I start a personal budget if I have never done it before?

Start by tracking your actual spending for one month without trying to change anything. Record every expense — bills, groceries, transport, dining out, everything. At the end of the month, review the totals. This gives you accurate data to build a realistic budget from, rather than guessing at numbers that may be far from reality.
