## 10. Teach Your Team the Same System

If you have staff who handle transactions, make sure they follow the same recording habits. Inconsistent entry styles — different date formats, missing customer names, vague notes — make your khata unreliable. A brief training session and a simple checklist can standardize the process across your team.
