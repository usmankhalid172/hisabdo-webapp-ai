## Automatic Calculations

Depending on the app, a digital ledger can reduce some of these difficulties. Check which functions it actually provides before relying on it:

A ledger app can calculate a running balance from entered transactions, reducing repeated manual addition. You still need to enter the correct amount, date and transaction type, and should review totals against source records regularly.
