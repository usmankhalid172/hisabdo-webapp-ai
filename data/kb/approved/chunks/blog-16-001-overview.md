## Overview

The case for offline-first financial tools — why reliability, performance and local data control make them a strong choice for many users, and what to understand about how data is actually stored.

The default assumption in modern software is that everything should be connected to the cloud. Your photos sync automatically. Your documents live on remote servers. Your messages pass through data centers on the other side of the world. For many applications, this makes sense. But for personal and business financial records, the offline-first approach offers significant advantages that cloud-dependent tools cannot always match.

This article explains those advantages honestly — including what "offline-first" actually means in practice, and where cloud features may still play a role.
