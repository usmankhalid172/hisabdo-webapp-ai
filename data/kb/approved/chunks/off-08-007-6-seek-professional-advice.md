## 6. Seek Professional Advice

If you are making significant financial, business or legal decisions, we strongly encourage you to seek advice from qualified professionals — including accountants, financial advisors, lawyers or tax consultants — who can provide guidance tailored to your specific circumstances.

HisabDo is a tool to support your record-keeping. It is not a substitute for professional expertise.
