## Key Bookkeeping Concepts (part 2)

### Accounts Receivable

Accounts receivable (AR) is money that customers owe you for goods or services you have already provided. Tracking AR accurately is essential for cash flow management — you need to know who owes you money and for how long. In khata terminology, this is the udhar that customers have taken from you.
