## 1. Record Every Transaction the Same Day

The most common mistake in khata management is delaying entries. When you wait until the end of the week to record transactions, you forget amounts, mix up customers and lose track of partial payments. Make it a rule: every transaction gets recorded the same day it happens.

If a customer buys on credit, note it immediately. If someone pays back a portion of what they owe, record that too. Consistency is more important than perfection.
