## Maintain a Payment Schedule

For businesses with multiple suppliers, a payment schedule is invaluable. This is simply a list of upcoming payments with their due dates, organized chronologically.

Review your payment schedule at the start of each week. Identify which payments need to be made that week and ensure you have sufficient cash to cover them. If you anticipate a cash shortfall, you have time to plan — perhaps by following up on customer receivables or arranging a short-term solution.
