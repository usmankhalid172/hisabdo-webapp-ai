## Is offline-first always more private than cloud apps?

Offline-only use (without signing in) means your data does not leave your device, which limits external exposure. However, "more private" depends on many factors including your device's own security, whether you use a backup service and how you share data. No app can guarantee absolute privacy or security. Review the Privacy Policy to understand exactly how your data is handled.
