## Step 1: Know Your Income

Start with what comes in. List every source of income you receive in a typical month: salary, freelance payments, business income, rental income, family support or any other regular source. Use your actual take-home amount, not your gross salary.

If your income varies month to month, use a conservative estimate — perhaps the average of your three lowest-income months in the past year. It is better to budget based on less and be pleasantly surprised than to budget based on more and fall short.
