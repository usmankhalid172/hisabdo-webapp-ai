## Local Records and Optional Sync

Guest mode stores supported records locally on your device. When signed in, supported customer, income and expense records may synchronize with Supabase when connected. The backup and restore process for local records is separate; see the [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for the current details.
