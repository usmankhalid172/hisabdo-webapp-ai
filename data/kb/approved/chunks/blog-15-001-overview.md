## Overview

Build a realistic budget that covers your essentials, savings goals and unexpected costs — without complicated spreadsheets or financial jargon.

A personal budget is simply a plan for how you will spend and save your money each month. It sounds straightforward, but most people either never create one or abandon it within a few weeks. The reason is usually that their budget is too rigid, too complicated or disconnected from how they actually live.

This guide takes a practical approach to personal budgeting — one that is flexible enough to work in real life and simple enough to maintain consistently.
