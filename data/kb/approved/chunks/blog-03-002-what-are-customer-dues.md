## What Are Customer Dues?

Customer dues — also called accounts receivable or udhar — are amounts that customers owe you for goods or services they have already received but not yet paid for. When you sell on credit, you are essentially lending money to your customer. The due is the outstanding loan balance.

In a traditional khata system, dues are recorded in a register against each customer's name. In a digital system, each customer has a profile with a complete transaction history and a running balance.
