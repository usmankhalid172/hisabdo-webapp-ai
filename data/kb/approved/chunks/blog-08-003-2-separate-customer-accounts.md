## 2. Separate Customer Accounts

A common error is keeping all transactions in one running list. Instead, maintain a separate account for each customer. This way you can instantly see what any individual owes you without scanning through pages of mixed entries.

In HisabDo, each customer gets their own profile with a complete transaction history and running balance. This makes it easy to answer the question "how much does Ahmed owe me?" in seconds rather than minutes.
