## Overview

Track invoices, client payments, subscriptions and business costs without confusion — practical financial management for independent workers and freelancers.

Freelancing offers freedom and flexibility, but it also comes with financial complexity that salaried employees never face. Variable income, multiple clients, business expenses, tax obligations and the constant need to track who has paid and who has not — these challenges require a more active approach to financial management than most freelancers initially expect.

This guide covers practical expense tracking strategies specifically designed for freelancers, from the solo developer to the independent consultant.
