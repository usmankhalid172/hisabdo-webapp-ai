## Should I pay suppliers early if I have the cash?

It depends on your situation. Paying early can strengthen supplier relationships and may earn you early payment discounts if the supplier offers them. However, if cash is tight, it is generally better to use your full payment terms and keep cash available for other needs. The key is to always pay within your agreed terms.
