## Plan for Tax Obligations

Freelancers are responsible for their own tax obligations, which vary significantly by country, jurisdiction and income level. Set aside a portion of every payment you receive in a dedicated account to cover future tax obligations. The appropriate percentage depends entirely on your local tax rules, income level and applicable deductions — consult a qualified tax professional in your jurisdiction for advice specific to your situation. Do not rely on generic percentages found online, as these vary widely and may not apply to you.
