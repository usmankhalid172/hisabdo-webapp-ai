## 2. Not Financial Advice

HisabDo is a financial record-keeping tool. It is not a financial advisor, accountant, tax consultant or legal advisor. Nothing in the HisabDo application, website, blog articles or any other content produced by HisabDo constitutes financial, accounting, investment, tax or legal advice.

The blog articles, guides and tips published on the HisabDo website are intended to provide general educational information about financial management practices. They are not tailored to your individual circumstances and should not be relied upon as professional advice.

For advice specific to your financial, tax or legal situation, please consult a qualified professional.
