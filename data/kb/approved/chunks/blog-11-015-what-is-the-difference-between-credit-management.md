## What is the difference between credit management and tracking customer dues?

Tracking customer dues is the mechanical process of recording every transaction and monitoring outstanding balances. Credit management is the broader policy and relationship side — deciding who gets credit, how much, on what terms, and how to handle overdue accounts. Both are necessary. See the related article on tracking customer dues for the recording mechanics.
