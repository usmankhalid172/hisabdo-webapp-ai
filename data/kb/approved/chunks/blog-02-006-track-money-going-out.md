## Track Money Going Out

Similarly, track every cash outflow with its date:

- Supplier payments
- Rent and utilities
- Staff salaries
- Loan repayments
- All other expenses

Pay particular attention to large, predictable outflows — rent, salaries, loan repayments. These happen on fixed dates and must be covered regardless of your sales performance that month.
