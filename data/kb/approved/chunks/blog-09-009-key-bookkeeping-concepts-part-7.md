## Key Bookkeeping Concepts (part 7)

### Reconciliation

Reconciliation is the process of comparing your records to an external source — typically your bank statement — to ensure they match. Regular reconciliation catches errors, identifies missing entries and confirms that your records are accurate. Most businesses reconcile monthly.
