## How much cash reserve should a small business keep?

A commonly cited guideline is 1–3 months of operating expenses as a cash reserve. This provides a buffer for slow periods, unexpected costs or delayed customer payments. Building this reserve takes time — start by setting aside a small amount each month until you reach your target.
