## Tracking Supplier Payables

Just as customers owe you money, you owe money to your suppliers. Track your supplier payables with the same care you give to customer receivables. Know what you owe, to whom and when it is due. Paying suppliers on time maintains good relationships and often qualifies you for better terms.
