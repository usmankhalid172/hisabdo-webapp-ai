## What is the most common bookkeeping mistake small businesses make?

Mixing personal and business finances is the most damaging mistake. When personal and business transactions share the same account or ledger, it becomes impossible to calculate true business costs, understand profitability or prepare accurate records for tax purposes. Even as a sole proprietor, maintain completely separate records for business and personal spending from day one.
