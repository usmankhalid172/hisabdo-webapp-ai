## Operating Expenses

Operating expenses are the day-to-day costs of running your business. They include everything required to keep the business functioning, excluding the direct cost of producing your product or service.

Common operating expenses include:

- Office or shop rent
- Utilities (electricity, gas, water)
- Internet and phone
- Office supplies and stationery
- Cleaning and maintenance
- Insurance
- Professional services (legal, accounting)
