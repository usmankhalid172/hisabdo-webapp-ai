## What is the simplest way to remember the difference between receivables and payables?

Receivables = money coming TO you (customers owe you). Payables = money going FROM you (you owe suppliers). If you are the one who gave goods on credit, it is a receivable. If you are the one who received goods on credit, it is a payable.
