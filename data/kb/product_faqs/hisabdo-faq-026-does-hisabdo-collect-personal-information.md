## Does HisabDo collect personal information?

HisabDo primarily processes information you enter directly in the app. Depending on how you use it, limited account or technical data may be handled by service providers such as Supabase, Google Play Services or advertising partners. See our [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy) for full details.
