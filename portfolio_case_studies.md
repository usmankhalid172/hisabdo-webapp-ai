## HisabDo

### Overview

HisabDo is an offline-first digital ledger for shopkeepers, freelancers, and
small businesses. It helps organize khata, expenses, customer records, and
receivables.

### Problem addressed

HisabDo aims to make daily financial record-keeping more practical than using
paper registers.

### Product approach

The portfolio describes an offline-first workflow, clear financial summaries,
and a simple interface for everyday use. It identifies the product's focus as
expense tracking, digital khata, and customer balances.

### Technology

- Flutter
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL

### Product materials

The portfolio page says screenshots of the dashboard, digital ledger, and
customer workflows are available there.
