## XICTEK Systems — About Us

## 1. Company Overview
XICTEK Systems is a product-focused software company that develops digital products and custom software solutions for businesses.
Tagline: Modern software. Real products. Business-focused solutions.

Its own product, HisabDo, gives the team experience in taking a product from design and development through release.
