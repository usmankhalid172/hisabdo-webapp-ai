## Company profile

XICTEK Systems is a product-focused software company founded by Mian Usman Khalid. It builds modern digital products and custom business software. Its own product is HisabDo.

## Product and engineering approach

The company focuses on useful products and practical solutions to real operational problems. Its stated approach is to build useful software and improve it over time through thoughtful iteration and feedback.

## Client communication

XICTEK Systems describes its client communication as direct and avoids making promises it cannot support.
