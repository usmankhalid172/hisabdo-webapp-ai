# HisabDo Product Overview

## What is HisabDo?

HisabDo is an offline-first khata and ledger app for shopkeepers, freelancers, small businesses, and other users who need to keep financial and customer records.

**Source:** [About HisabDo](https://hisabdo.app/about)

## What can users record?

The public product pages describe recording payments, dues, balances, customer accounts, transaction history, income, and expenses. The app is presented as a digital alternative to paper khata.

**Sources:** [About HisabDo](https://hisabdo.app/about), [HisabDo FAQ](https://hisabdo.app/faq)

## Who is it designed for?

The public pages name shopkeepers, freelancers, small business owners, home-based sellers, and service providers as intended users.

**Source:** [HisabDo FAQ](https://hisabdo.app/faq)

## Does it work offline?

The website says supported local features can be used offline. Internet access is required for online functions such as account access and cloud synchronization. The website does not state that every feature or record is available offline.

**Sources:** [HisabDo FAQ](https://hisabdo.app/faq), [Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Is it available on Android?

The website identifies HisabDo as an Android app and links to Google Play. Confirm the current minimum Android version against the live store listing before providing a version number; public and indexed page versions have differed.

**Sources:** [HisabDo FAQ](https://hisabdo.app/faq), [Google Play link on HisabDo](https://hisabdo.app/)
