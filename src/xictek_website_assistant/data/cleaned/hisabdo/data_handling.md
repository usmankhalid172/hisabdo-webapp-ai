# HisabDo Data Handling and Account Modes

## Guest mode and local records

The Privacy Policy says guest users can use supported local features without an account and keep supported records in local SQLite storage. It lists customers, income, expenses, settings, categories, and business profile information among information that may be stored locally.

**Source:** [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Signed-in synchronization

The Privacy Policy says supported customer, income, and expense records may synchronize through Supabase when a user is signed in and connected to the internet. It explicitly says the app does not claim that every locally stored category or record is synchronized.

**Source:** [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Internet-dependent actions

The Privacy Policy lists login, registration, cloud synchronization, and account recovery as actions that require an internet connection. Supported local guest functionality can be used offline.

**Source:** [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Backup and restore

The FAQ describes local backup to a device or supported external storage. The Privacy Policy distinguishes local backup/restore from cloud synchronization and says local backup is not a cloud restore mechanism. Create a backup before uninstalling or changing devices; the FAQ warns local data may be lost after uninstall if no backup was made.

**Sources:** [HisabDo FAQ](https://hisabdo.app/faq), [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Data security and third parties

The Privacy Policy names HTTPS communication, authentication, access control, database security rules, and Supabase as a service provider. It also says the website may use advertising technology and refers readers to the policy for data-handling details. This file does not claim a specific encryption algorithm or that the app is “air-gapped.”

**Source:** [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy)

## Sign-out and deletion

The Privacy Policy says signing out currently clears the local SQLite database on the device; cloud data associated with the account is not deleted merely by signing out. Treat the data-deletion page and current policy as the source for account deletion instructions.

**Sources:** [HisabDo Privacy Policy](https://hisabdo.app/privacy-policy), [Data Deletion](https://hisabdo.app/delete-data)
